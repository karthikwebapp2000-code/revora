Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we use

The core calculator needs a departure, destination and departure time. If you choose “Use current location”, your browser asks for permission and supplies your location to the app for the calculation.

Guest use

This starter version does not require an account to calculate a journey. We do not put your location into a personal profile.

Third-party services

Place search is provided through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and route calculation through OSRM. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.

Future accounts

If accounts, saved routes, analytics or other services are added, this policy should be updated before production launch to accurately describe the data collected and retention periods.

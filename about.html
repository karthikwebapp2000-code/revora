About Rayvora

Finding a more comfortable seat, one journey at a time.

How it works

Rayvora combines your route, departure time and the position of the sun to estimate which side of the vehicle is likely to receive less direct sunlight. It is designed for everyday bus, train and road journeys.

A simple idea

Hot, sunny journeys can make a normal commute uncomfortable. Rayvora turns a small amount of route and solar information into a simple choice: left or right.

Important note

The result is an estimate. Buildings, trees, road curves, vehicle orientation, window tint and weather can affect actual sunlight. Always use your own judgment while traveling.

Built for the web

The app works without an account for the core calculation. Location search and routing use public mapping services in this starter build.
